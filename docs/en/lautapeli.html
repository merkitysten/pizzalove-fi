Before your first game

The box contains a sticker sheet and four boxes that are assembled once. It takes a moment, and after that the game is always ready to play.

How the game works

Winning the game

Win by being the first to collect 10 different slices in your box. Make pizzas on the board to earn matching slices for your box. A heart pizza gives you one extra slice of your choice.

Game preparations

  • Stack the colourful topping towers in the storage.
  • Shuffle and place 4 pizza cards on the board. Place the remaining pizza cards as a deck on the table.
  • Shuffle and place the monkey cards as a deck on the table.
  • Give each player an empty box.

Taking a turn

  1. Rolling the dice and moving the pieces

    The player who last ate pizza starts by rolling the dice. The dice determines how many pieces are moved between the storage and the line. Same-coloured pieces always move as one tower. If you cannot make a move, your turn ends.

    The monkey scales and weights help children visualise the moves. They do not need to be used.

  2. Pizza making

    When the toppings a pizza needs are on the line, make the pizza and collect the slices into your box. You can make several pizzas per turn, but each topping can only be used for one pizza per turn. Return the used toppings to the storage and draw a new pizza card from the deck to the board.

  3. Picking up a monkey card

    If you made at least one pizza, pick up a monkey card at the end of your turn and follow it. The turn moves clockwise.

Three ways to play

Rolling the dice, moving toppings and making pizzas work the same way in all three.

Pizza Love – basic gameA race for the whole family, 6+

Make the pizza boxes’ favourite pizzas and be the first to collect 10 different slices in your box. Once you know the basic game, the rule book has extra rules to make it faster.

Hungry MonkeysCooperative game, for ages about 4–5

The easiest way to start. You play together and fill a shared box. Fill your box with 10 different slices and you all win. But watch out: the hungry monkeys eat their slices straight away if no new ones arrive!

Secret PizzasExciting and strategic

Everyone gets two secret pizzas that the others can’t see – so no one knows which pizzas you’re going to make. The first to fill their box with 10 different slices wins.
