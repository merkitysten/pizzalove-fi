The game in the bag: Topping Ten

Print the board, tip the toppings onto the table and play. The rules are on this page.

2–4 players · ages 7+ · about 15 minutes

In the bag

  • 55 toppings in ten colours, values 1–10
  • A ten-sided die
  • A Basso tattoo

Download the board

The board is one A4 sheet. The line version is the same game but uses less ink.

Your turn

1

Roll the die.

2

Take toppings from the counter worth exactly the number you rolled.

3

Place them on the empty slices of your pizza, one topping per slice.

Roll a 10 and you take one ten, or 1 + 2 + 3 + 4. The value is the same either way, but the ten fills one slice and the four small ones fill four.

You cannot take more than you have empty slices.

Goal

Build the most valuable pizza. The game ends when someone fills their pizza, but value decides the winner, not speed.

Setup

Each player takes one pizza. The toppings go on their own places on the counter.

If you cannot or will not take

You pass. Three passes in a row and you are out.

Who starts the round

The round starts with whoever has the fewest toppings. If that is tied, the one whose toppings are worth least.

End of the game

The game ends the moment someone has filled all ten slices. That player has not won yet.

Scoring

Everyone adds up the value of the toppings on their pizza. The most valuable pizza wins.

A tie

If two pizzas are worth the same, the one with fewer toppings wins. If that is equal too, the win is shared.

A tip

Small toppings are scarce: one 1 and two 2s. They are what lets you hit the sum exactly when no single topping fits, and a missed turn is worth nothing at all. Big toppings save slices, and you only have ten.

Variants

Put one back

If you cannot make your roll, put one topping back on the counter and try again.

Exactly 30

Slices no longer limit you, value does. You win when your toppings are worth exactly 30. You may not go over, so take the largest that fits.
