The game that comes with the bag: Topping Ten

A bag of toppings, a ten-sided die and a Basso tattoo. The board and the rules are free: download, print and play.

2–4 players · ages 7+ · about 15 minutes

In the bag

  • 55 toppings in ten colours, values 1–10
  • A ten-sided die
  • A Basso tattoo

Download the board

One A4 sheet: four pizzas and the counter the toppings come from. The colour version looks better, the line version saves ink.

  • Board, colourPDF 1,2 Mt
  • Board, line versionPDF 487 kt

Your turn

1

Roll the die.

2

Take toppings from the counter worth exactly the number you rolled.

3

Place them on the empty slices of your pizza, one topping per slice.

A roll of 10 gets you one 10, or 1 + 2 + 3 + 4. One ten fills one slice; four small ones fill four.

You cannot take more toppings than you have empty slices.

Goal

Be the first to fill all ten slices of your pizza.

Setup

Each player takes one pizza. The toppings go on their numbered places on the counter.

If you cannot or will not take

You pass. Three passes in a row and you are out.

Who starts the round

Whoever has the fewest toppings on their pizza. If several are tied, the one whose toppings are worth least.

Winning

The first player with a full pizza wins.

A tip

Small toppings are scarce: one 1, two 2s. They are what makes an exact sum possible. Once they are gone from the counter, many rolls go to waste.

Variants

Put one back

If you cannot make your roll, put one of your toppings back on the counter and try again.

Exactly 30

Slices no longer limit you; value decides. You win when your toppings are worth exactly 30. You may not go over: take the largest combination that fits.
